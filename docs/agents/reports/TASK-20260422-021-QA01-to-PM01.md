## Observations (non-blocking)

1. **Vacant-room edit mode:** Clicking **Edit / Add Patient Information** on a vacant room routes through `handleOpenUpdateDialog` (`isUpdateMode=true`) rather than `handleOpenAdmitDialog` (`isUpdateMode=false`). The dialog still opens and the bed is pre-selected, but the title reads `Update Patient: Vacant`. Recommend a follow-up polish to route vacant rooms through admit mode; not a release blocker for occupied-patient primary flow.
2. **Smoke coverage gap:** `qa-smoke.spec.ts` does not yet click into patient details or assert the new button. Manual/code verification used for this task; consider extending smoke in a future harness task.
